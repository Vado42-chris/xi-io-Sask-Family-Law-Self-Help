# Legal Information Boundary v1

Status: `mandatory product boundary, not legal review`

## Allowed assistance

The product may:

- reproduce and explain approved public procedural information,
- ask questions required by a versioned workflow,
- organize user-provided facts,
- identify missing information and records,
- generate editable draft language from confirmed facts,
- compare documents for internal consistency,
- prepare a package for user or professional review,
- identify when a specialized legal question requires referral.

## Prohibited or gated assistance

The product must not present itself as determining:

- whether a user will win,
- which legal claim is strategically best,
- the credibility of a person,
- the truth of disputed allegations,
- whether evidence is admissible,
- whether service or filing is legally effective without verified facts,
- whether a deadline has been satisfied when calculation inputs are uncertain,
- whether a user should sign, swear, affirm, consent, settle, or waive a right.

## Content labels

Every meaningful statement should be classifiable as:

- `official_source`,
- `deterministic_workflow_rule`,
- `user_provided_fact`,
- `uploaded_evidence_reference`,
- `ai_suggestion`,
- `human_approved_draft`,
- `professional_reviewed`,
- `court_issued`,
- `unknown`.

The interface must not visually collapse these categories.

## Escalation triggers

Trigger a specialized safety and referral state for family or interpersonal violence, immediate danger, child safety, criminal proceedings, Child and Family Services, protection orders, unsafe contact or service, imminent deadlines, incapacity, language-access barriers, or a source conflict.

## Professional review

The system should make professional review easy by exporting a concise matter summary, unresolved questions, source list, form package, and change history. It must not state that a document is lawyer-reviewed unless the reviewer and scope are recorded.

## Disclaimer behavior

A disclaimer does not cure unsafe product behaviour. The product must enforce boundaries through workflow design, permissions, labels, source controls, and review gates.
